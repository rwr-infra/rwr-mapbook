Notes

Note: template version vao0822. The left preview image is an oblique view looking down, the right one faces the normal directly — that is, the editor view. Judge the shape by the horizontal line; the vertical one is in perspective and can make an object look tilted. And whatever you do, don't lump every material error under "the template points at a restricted file"; errors like the fir tree and the placard are definitely not that — they are another kind of reference-parameter error :/

Data & privacy

This site collects no visitor data. There is no analytics, no advertising, no cookie and no third-party request of any kind. Fonts, styles and scripts are all self-hosted, so any page contacts this site’s domain only. A single light/dark display preference is stored locally by the browser, and is removed when browser data is cleared.
